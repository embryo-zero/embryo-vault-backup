# Embryo Vault Backup

Embryo Vault Backup is Embryo-Zero's fork of [borg](https://github.com/borgbackup/borg) (6953bfa, commit 6953bfa). It does deduplicating archiver with compression and encryption.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
